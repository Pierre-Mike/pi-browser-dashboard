## ★ TOP — Stale worktree branches never pruned after squash-merge

- **Signal:** 55 local branches; ~40 are `worktree-*`. Most show "1 ahead of main" — the
  signature of a squash-merged feature (squash rewrites SHA, so the original branch reads as
  ahead even though its changes are on `main`). Confirmed merged & deletable:
  `worktree-git-pull-button` (0 ahead), `worktree-harden-terminal-cwd` (0), `worktree-ui-test-extension` (0),
  plus every 1-ahead single-commit worktree branch (`worktree-spawn-effort`, `worktree-orchestration-tab`,
  `worktree-home-ui-daisy`, `worktree-ext-local-root`, `worktree-fix-tree-and-diff`, …).
- **Hypothesis:** `EnterWorktree` workflow creates a branch per task; nothing deletes it after the
  PR squash-merges. Cruft accumulates and obscures genuinely in-flight branches.
- **Proposed action:** `script` `scripts/prune-merged-worktrees.sh` — for each `worktree-*` branch,
  if `git cherry main <branch>` reports all commits as already-applied (`-` lines only), or the
  branch is 0-ahead, delete the branch and remove its `.claude/worktrees/` checkout. Dry-run by
  default, `--force` to delete. Optionally wire into Lefthook `post-merge`.
- **Enforcement type:** `script`

- **Enforcement type:** `script` (soft — depends on `gh` auth)
